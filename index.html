01 / The idea

A pass, not a free pass.

Bot protection asks who gets in. Ultipass is about what happens next. Behavior, not just identity.

01 / Ground rules ⌘

The site calls the shots.

Rate limits. Hold limits. Allowed actions. Site owners define what good agent behavior looks like, in rules a machine can follow.

02 / Receipts ≋

No trust-me-bro logs.

The plan: signed sessions and a tamper-evident action ledger. If something goes sideways, start with evidence, not finger-pointing.

03 / Real backup ↗

Responsibility has teeth.

We're designing an operator-funded bond and insurance model to cover documented losses from rule violations, subject to agreed terms.

02 / The proposed flight path

Not an invisibility cloak.
A reason to open the door.

  1. 01 / Declare

    Set the house rules.

    The site publishes what agents may and may not do.

  2. 02 / Identify

    Know who's visiting.

    An identified operator stands behind the agent session.

  3. 03 / Enforce & record

    Keep the receipts.

    The gateway enforces rules and records agent actions.

  4. 04 / Resolve

    Back the behavior.

    Use evidence to assess a violation and any covered loss.

This is the model we're building, not a live service. Sites stay in control of access. Coverage would depend on policy terms and eligibility.
