What it is

Agents are already on your site. Nobody is on the hook.

Bot protection decides who gets in. Ultipass covers what happens once they are inside: it prices behavior, not bytes.

01

Rules

Sites declare what agents may do: rate, holds, scope. Rules are machine-readable and enforced where the session lives.

02

Evidence

Each action is signed and written to a tamper-evident ledger, so a dispute starts from facts instead of logs and guesses.

03

Insurance

Agent operators back their agents with a bond. When a documented violation costs a site money, the loss is paid out.

How it works

From rule to payout, with a paper trail.

 

STEP 1
Declare

The site publishes its rules for agents.

STEP 2
Identify

The operator signs every agent session.

STEP 3
Record

Actions land in a hash-chained ledger.

STEP 4
Settle

Proven loss is paid from the bond.

Who it is for

Both sides of the door.

 

Site owners

Open the door to useful agents without absorbing the cost of bad ones.

  • Write the rules once
  • Get evidence you can act on
  • Be paid for documented loss

Agent operators

Show sites you are accountable, and get access that bot walls would otherwise deny.

  • Verifiable agent identity
  • Clear, testable rules
  • One bond instead of one-off deals
